# Style Guide: Adam

## The thesis
This wardrobe is built on the relaxed elegance of 1960s Milan, as requested. It uses a tight, warm palette and soft, natural fabrics to complement your skin tone and physique. The silhouette is slim but never tight, prioritising comfort through higher-rise trousers and unconstructed tailoring. It is a move away from generic casualwear towards a timeless, coherent look that is sharp but effortless. Every piece is chosen to accentuate your athletic frame, not hide it.

## What it should say
**On dates:** You look confident and considered, but not like you tried too hard. The soft fabrics and comfortable fit say you are at ease with yourself. The look is masculine and a little romantic, showing an appreciation for quality that your girlfriend will notice.

**At work:** You are sharp, competent, and classic. Your clothes are an elevated form of casual that signals professionalism without needing the formality of a traditional business suit. The coherent palette and consistent fit project an image of someone who is organised and has an eye for detail.

## What it must never say
*   Sloppy
*   Generic
*   Synthetic
*   Try-hard
*   Ill-fitting

## Silhouette and fit
Your lean, athletic frame is an asset. The goal is to drape it, not constrict it.

*   **Trousers:** Mid-to-high rise is non-negotiable. This solves your shirt-tucking problem and creates a more elegant, elongated leg line. The fit should be slim through the thigh with a gentle taper to the ankle. Aim for no break or a single, slight break. Single or double pleats will provide more comfort and room through the seat and thigh, which is your highest priority.
*   **Shirts and Tops:** Slim fit, but with room to move. The shoulder seam must sit perfectly on your shoulder bone. The body should follow the V-shape of your torso without pulling at the buttons. The sleeve should end at your wrist bone.
*   **Jackets and Outerwear:** Unstructured or soft shoulders are essential. They will follow your natural shoulder line instead of artificially padding it. The jacket should be tailored for a nipped-in waist to accentuate your physique.

## Fabric and texture
You love natural fabrics, and so does this aesthetic. They are more comfortable and age better than synthetics.

*   **Embrace:** Linen, brushed cotton, and wool are your core. Add high-twist 'fresco' wool for breathable summer trousers, merino wool for fine-gauge knits, and cashmere blends for luxurious comfort. Suede for jackets and shoes adds textural depth.
*   **Avoid:** All synthetics like polyester and acrylic. They look cheap, feel uncomfortable, and do not align with your 'timeless' goal. Avoid heavy, stiff denim that fights your frame; if you wear jeans, make them a softer, lighter wash in a slim-tapered cut.

## Retire

*   **Oversize heavyweight synthetic t-shirt in salmon:** The colour is wrong for your palette, the synthetic fabric is uncomfortable, and the fit does not accentuate your physique.
*   **Old t-shirts and jeans:** This is your "slob" uniform. It does not align with your goal of looking sharp and sophisticated.
*   **Black Oxfords:** You never wear them because they are too formal and do not fit the warm, relaxed aesthetic we are building. This wardrobe uses brown suede and leather.

## Shopping list
Your low score for Cost means you should prioritise quality over quantity. Buy less, but buy better.

1.  **High-Rise Pleated Trousers (Wool):** £150-300. Look for a pair in chocolate or navy. This is the most urgent purchase as it establishes your new silhouette and solves your main fit problem. It unlocks the *Weekday Edit* and *Gigolo* uniforms.
2.  **Brown Suede Loafers:** £150-400. The single most important shoe. It is more comfortable and versatile than the Oxfords you own. This unlocks almost every uniform.
3.  **Knitted Polos (x2):** £80-150 each. Buy one in cream and one in navy or burnt orange. This is your new, sharp replacement for the old t-shirt, immediately elevating your casual look for dates and weekends. Comfort is your priority, and these deliver.
4.  **Unstructured Blazer (Tobacco):** £300-600. This is the hero piece you specifically mentioned wanting. Look for linen, cotton, or a wool blend with no shoulder padding. This unlocks the *Gigolo* and provides a sharp outer layer for dates.
5.  **High-Quality T-shirts (3-pack):** £60-100. Find a brand with a good slim fit in cream or stone. This upgrades your most basic garment and provides the base layer for many outfits.

## Rules of thumb
*   If the rise isn't high, don't buy.
*   Natural fabrics only. Texture is your friend.
*   Warm tones first: tobacco, cream, chocolate, olive.
*   The shoulder seam sits on the bone. Always.
*   Fit is everything. Find a good tailor and use them.
*   When in doubt: brown suede loafers.
